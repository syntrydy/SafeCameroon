# Prompt 10 - Organization Console

Read `docs/PRD.md`, `docs/MVP_FLOWS.md`, and `docs/SECURITY_PRIVACY.md`.

Build a simple React/TypeScript organization console.

Screens:
- login;
- review queue;
- case detail;
- alert preview;
- subscriptions;
- delivery history;
- organization members/roles.

Keep UI intentionally simple. Prioritize traceability and safe defaults over visual complexity.
