# Prompt 15 - Pilot/Hackathon Demo

Create a deterministic demo scenario that shows:

1. anonymous citizen submits a missing-child report;
2. AI extracts candidate information;
3. reviewer sees the report and confirms a case;
4. verified case generates a community-safe alert;
5. police, NGO, association, and citizen subscriptions match differently;
6. deliveries go through different channels;
7. provider failure triggers fallback;
8. child-found resolution generates a follow-up update;
9. audit/history explains every critical decision.

The demo must make the platform nature obvious: adding another consumer or another channel should not require changing the core incident logic.
